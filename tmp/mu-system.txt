<div class="mu-case-heading"><span class="mu-case-kicker">Foundations</span><h2>Design system.</h2><div class="mu-case-heading-body"><p>I built and maintained Muthabr’s design system in Figma, with reusable components, variants, styles and variables, following Material Design and Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines. One system kept the app, web app, website and emails consistent, and made the post-launch features (referrals and pay-by-subject) faster to design and build.</p></div></div>
<div class="mu-ds">
<ul class="mu-ds-stats">
<li><b>2</b>variable collections</li><li><b>96</b>variables</li><li><b>15</b>text styles</li><li><b>~250</b>components and variants</li><li><b>17</b>icons</li>
</ul>
